# Published manual times: references

The 17 published studies behind the "published" column of the manual-time baseline: how long experts take, by hand, to outline a lung tumour or nodule, measure its long and short axis, re-find it on a follow-up CT, and record the result. Numbered 1–17 for citation.

**By step:**
- **Outline (3D, by hand):** 1–9.
- **Long and short axis:** 10–13.
- **Re-finding on a follow-up:** 9, 11, 14–16.
- **Recording:** 11–13, 17. No study times recording on its own.

**How the numbers were read:** each time was read in the paper's open full text or its abstract. References 13–16 were read as abstracts only. Reference 17 was checked and reports no time. Numbers seen only in secondary sources (trade press, conference posters) are left out.
