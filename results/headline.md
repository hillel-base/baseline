## Time saved

- **Main figure (b), measured plus modelled corrections:** a median of **2.3 min per scan re-timed for a standard PC** (slowest 4.9 min; the lab's computer has no graphics card) and **1.3 min on this Mac** (slowest 2.7 min), against the lab's **20–34 min** per scan with a volume (5–9 min without one). That is with up to 5 corrections toward the expert's outline, modelled by script from the operator's own click (median 2), each costing his own placement time plus the model's. 18 runs. Labelled: an efficient corrector steered by the expert; a real expert decides when to stop and may click more or less. **A lower bound on the tool's time, so its time saved is the most to expect:** the corrector is an oracle that knows where the error is, each correction costs only a click on a point shown on screen (his median 11.7 s) plus the model, and nothing is reviewed after it. Steered toward the radiologists instead: 1.1 min (Mac) and 2.0 min (PC) per scan, 16 runs. (`projection.csv`, `projection_r2.csv`)
- **Floor (a), measured only:** one click, no corrections: 44 s per scan (28–64 s) on the Mac. Not a saving on its own, because most one-click outlines are not good enough (below). With failures left out: 39 s (10 runs).
- **Per step, (b), against the lab's and the published times:** volume 15–25 min (published 2–22 min) → 51 s Mac, 115 s PC; axes 2–4 min (published about 40 s) → 52 s Mac, 115 s PC, slower than the published time; record 3–5 min → 14 s. ## The main failure

One click is not enough on hard tumours. All 8 failures are both scans of 4 patients: on two small tumours the tool takes in the hazy area around them (+95% to +181% in volume against the expert), and on two large tumours touching other tissue it stays in part of the tumour (−48% to −78%). Irregular tumours (4 patients, 8 scans, by our fixed rule) still miss the expert's typical change after 5 modelled corrections in 2 of 8 scans from his click (3 of 8 from the deepest point, by script). Where the click lands also matters: 22 of 141 clicks 3–5 mm off the centre gave over twice the expert's volume, and 27 under half. (`scores.csv`, `replay.csv`, `q3_summary_timed.csv`)
